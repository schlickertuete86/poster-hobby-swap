# Hobby Hopper Listings

Design a "browse listings" screen for Hobby Hopper, an app where people give away and receive unused hobby/craft materials (yarn, paint, beads, fabric scraps). Show a header with the app name, a search bar, and a grid of 6 material listing cards (each with a small thumbnail, title, short description, category tag, and "Anfragen" button). 

VISUAL STYLE — bold, experimental, high-contrast, editorial poster energy: Background: a full-bleed, flowing abstract gradient — organic marbled swirls, NOT geometric blobs or circles. Blend warm burnt-coral (#E2704A) into cool periwinkle-blue (#8B9FE8), with occasional near-black (#0A0A0A) and near-white (#F7F4EC) passages swirling through for contrast — like liquid marbling or a Riso print. Add a fine film-grain/noise texture over the whole background. Colors should sometimes blend smoothly, sometimes clash/contrast sharply — irregular and painterly, never symmetric. Foreground UI — strict Neo-Bauhaus, totally separate from the background's chaos: sharp, unrounded corners on every card, button and input (no border-radius anywhere). Solid geometric accent shapes (circle, triangle, square) used as small icons or dividers. Thin 1px rule lines as section dividers. A radiating sunburst/starburst line icon as a recurring motif. Typography: heavy condensed all-caps display font (like Anton) for the headline, clean grotesk (like Space Grotesk) for body text and buttons, monospace (like IBM Plex Mono) for small tags/meta labels. Overall mood: loud, confident, print-poster, not a typical soft SaaS app.
